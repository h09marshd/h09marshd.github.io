Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who we are: Witchwood is made by Busnify Ltd, duane.marsh20@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your game (your cottage, garden, coins and everything you make) is saved on your phone. If you're signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your own Google account, so it can follow you to a new phone. We don't have a server and we never receive your save.
  • If the game crashes, a crash report is sent to Google's Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. It doesn't include your name or email.
  • Play stats (which parts of the game are played) are only sent if you say yes. You're asked once, and you can change it any time at the record player in the game.
  • If you buy something, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details.
  • If you choose to watch an ad for a free boost, Google AdMob shows it. Some of those ads come from Google's partners AppLovin and Unity Ads. Ads are never forced. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Shop → Offers & coins → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the App Store handles payments, and there are no crash reports, play stats or cloud save (see "On iPhone and iPad" below).
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't build profiles about you.

What is collected, by whom, and why

By Witchwood itself

Your progress and settings are stored in the game's private storage on your device. If Android backup is on, Android may back these files up to your Google account (that is between you and Google; you can turn it off in your phone's settings).

Google Play Games (cloud save)

If you're signed in to Google Play Games, the game keeps a copy of your save in Google Play Games' Saved Games, in your own Google account, and reads it back to offer your game on a new phone or tablet. It never replaces the game on your phone without asking you. The save holds only your game: your cottage, garden, coins and progress. We never receive it. Google Play Games' own profile and sign-in are covered by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can delete your Play Games data for Witchwood in the Google Play Games settings.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

If the game crashes, a report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains what went wrong in the game's code, your device model, Android version, the game's version, and a random ID for this installation of the game. It doesn't contain your name, email, advertising ID or your save. Reports are kept by Google for 90 days. This is in our legitimate interest in keeping the game working.

Firebase Analytics (play stats, only if you say yes)

If you choose to share play stats, Firebase Analytics, a Google service, records how the game is used: for example which parts are played, how long a session lasts, your device model, the game's version and your approximate country (from your IP address), with a random ID for this installation of the game. It never uses your advertising ID, is never used for adverts or ad personalisation, and isn't linked to your name or email. Data is kept for 2 months. It is off until you say yes, and you can turn it off at any time at the record player in the game.

Google Play (purchases)

When you buy something, Google Play processes the payment and tells the game what you bought, so it can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google AdMob (rewarded ads, only when you choose)

You only ever see an ad when you choose to watch one. So that one is ready when you do, the game starts Google AdMob when it opens and loads an ad in the background. When it does, and when an ad plays, Google AdMob may collect device information, including your phone's advertising ID, your IP address (used for approximate location), and how you interacted with the ad. This is used to show the ad, measure it, and prevent fraud, and, where you agree, to personalise ads. In the UK, the EEA and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Shop → Offers & coins → Ad privacy choices. Google's explanation: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

AppLovin and Unity Ads (through Google AdMob)

When Google has no ad ready, some of the ads you choose to watch come from AppLovin or Unity Ads, which work with Google AdMob. When they show an ad they may collect the same kinds of device information as above (your advertising ID where you allow it, your IP address for approximate location, and how you interacted with the ad), to show and measure the ad and to prevent fraud. Your consent choice in the game is passed on to them. Their privacy policies: AppLovin https://www.applovin.com/privacy/ and Unity https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

On iPhone and iPad

On an iPhone or iPad, Witchwood is the same game with fewer services:

  • Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the game what you bought, so it can unlock it and restore it (Restore purchases). Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Ads work as described above (Google AdMob, with AppLovin and Unity Ads), with the same consent choices. Witchwood never asks to track you, so the ads never receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No crash reports, play stats or cloud save are sent from an iPhone or iPad. Your save stays on the device, and in your own iCloud or computer backup if you use one.
  • Reminders are only set on your device, if you allow notifications. Nothing is sent anywhere.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads; on an iPhone or iPad, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, where Witchwood never appears because it never asks).

Children

Witchwood is intended for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

  • Ads: never required to play. Change your consent from Shop → Offers & coins → Ad privacy choices.
  • Play stats: off unless you say yes; switch them at the record player in the game.
  • Cloud save: only while you're signed in to Google Play Games; your Play Games data can be deleted in Google Play Games' settings.
  • Your save: stays on your device; uninstalling the game deletes it (unless Android backup or Google Play Games kept a copy in your Google account).
  • Questions or requests: email duane.marsh20@gmail.com. If you are in the UK or EU you have rights under data protection law (access, deletion, objection); because we hold no data about you ourselves, requests about ads or purchases are best made to Google, but we will help.

Changes
